Who We Are

Passionate About Every Journey

Founded in 2009, Wanderlust Travel Agency was born from a simple belief: every person deserves to experience the world's most extraordinary places — without the stress of planning. What started as a small boutique agency in New York has grown into a global travel powerhouse, with offices in 12 countries and a team of 200+ certified travel specialists.

We've sent over 50,000 travelers to 120+ destinations across 6 continents. Each trip is personally crafted, meticulously planned, and backed by our 24/7 support guarantee. From honeymoon escapes to family adventures and solo backpacking — we've seen it all and planned it perfectly.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Everything we do is guided by a clear purpose: to make extraordinary travel accessible and effortless for everyone.

🎯
Our Mission

To inspire and empower travelers of all backgrounds to explore the world with confidence. We are committed to delivering exceptional travel experiences through expert guidance, personalized service, and unwavering attention to detail. We believe every journey should be a story worth telling.

🌍
Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted travel companion — a company where every client feels like our only client. We envision a world where cultural exchange through travel builds bridges of understanding, empathy, and global citizenship. By 2030, we aim to connect 500,000 travelers with transformative experiences.
